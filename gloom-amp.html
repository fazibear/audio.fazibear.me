Low-tuned amplification

A darker kind of heavy.

Gloom Amp is a virtual amplifier for low-tuned guitars. Dial in the drive, shape the lows, mids, and highs, and adjust presence to bring your tone into focus.

Controls

Shape your sound.

Input

Set the signal level going into the amplifier.

Drive

Adjust the amount of gain and distortion.

Low, mid & high

Shape the low-end weight, midrange character, and high-end bite with three dedicated EQ controls.

Presence

Adjust the upper-frequency character of your amplifier tone.

Output

Set the level leaving the amplifier to fit the rest of your signal chain.
