The engine

Real MP3 compression.
Real artifacts.

Llamafier adds the sound of lossy MP3 compression by actually encoding and decoding your audio through the LAME codec. Bring those compression artifacts into your mix and use them as part of the texture.

Controls

Two controls. Lossy character.

Bitrate

Set the MP3 bitrate. Lower settings push the compression harder and make its artifacts more apparent; higher settings preserve more of the source.

Quality

Adjust the LAME encoder quality setting to explore how the codec handles your sound at the selected bitrate.
