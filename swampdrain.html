The engine

Keep the weight.
Lose the mud.

Low tunings create energy exactly where dense mixes become crowded. SwampDrain controls that buildup, restores pick definition, and keeps individual notes readable without thinning out the performance.

Controls

Four controls. Instant focus.

Input

Set the level hitting the plugin and match it to your source.

Output

Return a controlled, mix-ready signal to your channel.

Drain

Remove the low-mid buildup that hides definition in down-tuned parts.

Murk

Add intensity and harmonic weight without losing note separation.
