Metal distortion

Heavy tone. Your way.

Inspired by the Metal Zone, MT666 puts distortion and tone shaping at your fingertips. Adjust the lows and highs, then use the middle level and frequency controls to find your place in the mix.

Controls

Dial in the aggression.

Input level

Set the signal level going into the distortion.

Distortion

Dial in the amount of distortion for your guitar tone.

Output level

Adjust the level leaving the plugin.

Low & high

Shape the low-end weight and high-end bite with separate EQ controls.

Middle

Boost or cut the midrange to shape the character of your sound.

Middle frequency

Choose where the middle EQ works, from 200 Hz to 5 kHz.
